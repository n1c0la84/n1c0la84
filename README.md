### Hi, I'm Nicola 👋

I like, with the help of AI (mostly Claude Code), making hardware do things it wasn't sold for: Linux on ARM laptops, kernel patches, reverse engineering, and car and radio tinkering. I test everything on the real hardware myself. Computer science graduate, based in Rome.

**Now**

- 🐧 [**book4-edge-linux**](https://github.com/n1c0la84/book4-edge-linux): Linux (Arch + Omarchy, Fedora) on the Samsung Galaxy Book4 Edge 14" (Snapdragon X Elite). Boots at EL2 with KVM, deep sleep at about 0.9 W, four speakers, camera, fingerprint reader, and charging through the Samsung EC. The kernel patches are in [linux-book4-edge](https://github.com/n1c0la84/linux-book4-edge).
- 📡 [**flipper-hyundai-tpms**](https://github.com/n1c0la84/flipper-hyundai-tpms): a standalone Flipper Zero app that reads Hyundai Continental/VDO tyre-pressure sensors.
- 🎵 [**omarchy-ytmusic**](https://github.com/n1c0la84/omarchy-ytmusic): YouTube Music in the Omarchy bar, played through mpv with no browser window.
